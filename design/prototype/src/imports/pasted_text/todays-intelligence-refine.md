PRIMARY GOAL

Transform Today’s Intelligence into a premium editorial overview of the current research day.

The section should immediately answer:

• What is happening in today’s research?
• Which competitions and research themes are most active?
• Where should the user look next?

It should feel like the opening spread of a premium football intelligence publication.

Not a dashboard.

Not a statistics grid.

Not a KPI section.

INFORMATION HIERARCHY

Create a clear three-level hierarchy:

1. Primary intelligence story
2. Supporting intelligence signals
3. Secondary contextual details

Do not give every item equal visual weight.

One item must lead.

The remaining items must support it.

The user’s eye should have an obvious starting point.

PRIMARY INTELLIGENCE STORY

Use the strongest real intelligence item already present in the section as the lead.

Make it larger and more editorial.

It may include:

• competition identity
• real fixture or research context
• real qualification state
• real market or provider metric
• last updated information

Do not invent any new metric, score or copy.

Use only existing data.

The lead item should feel like a research story, not a large card.

Avoid a heavy bordered container.

Use typography, alignment, spacing and competition atmosphere to create hierarchy.

SUPPORTING INTELLIGENCE

Supporting items should become compact editorial entries.

Each entry should contain only:

• competition or fixture identity
• one primary research fact
• one short contextual line
• one clear path to explore

Do not display multiple competing statistics in one entry.

Do not use identical cards.

Do not create a grid of equal boxes.

Use structured rows, columns or plates with clear editorial rhythm.

COMPETITION IDENTITY

Use the established League DNA system.

For each competition:

• official competition logo
• restrained competition colour
• subtle left-to-right colour wash
• optional cropped watermark only on the lead item
• thin colour cue on supporting entries

Competition identity must help scanning.

It must never overpower the information.

League = atmosphere.

Club = secondary accent.
