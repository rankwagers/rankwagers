# Vision

RankWagers must never become a generic "AI betting tips" website. Instead it should become an **Evidence-First Football Intelligence Platform** where AI **explains, ranks, and summarizes** statistically verified evidence.

- The AI never invents picks.
- The evidence engine remains authoritative.
- AI explains and prioritizes.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Evidence First
Every AI recommendation must originate from the existing evidence system. AI cannot create a prediction from nothing. Required inputs (where applicable): FootyStats statistics · Evidence Capture · Validation history · Settlement history · Odds · Historical accuracy · Qualification engine · Data freshness · Operator availability.

## 2. Deterministic Selection
Selection logic remains deterministic. AI does NOT decide what is selected.

```
Qualification Engine → Evidence Ranking Engine → Candidate Selection → AI Explanation
```

LLM output is never the authoritative selector.

## 3. Explainable AI
Every recommendation must explain: why selected · why confidence is high · why alternatives were rejected · risk factors · supporting statistics · historical evidence. No black-box recommendations.

# AI Milestones

## AI-1 — Evidence Ranking Engine
Rank all qualified candidates. Outputs: `predictionScore`, `confidenceScore`, `riskScore`, `valueScore`, `historicalReliability`. No user-facing UI yet.

## AI-2 — AI Bet of the Day
Generate at most one daily recommendation. **If confidence is insufficient → produce "No qualified Bet of the Day" instead of forcing a pick.** (Fail-closed; increases long-term trust.)

## AI-3 — AI Match Predictions
Generate only qualified predictions. Markets (future-expandable): 1X2 · Double Chance · Over/Under · BTTS · Draw No Bet · Corners · Cards. Predictions below the qualification threshold are omitted.

## AI-4 — AI Acca
Two independent accumulator products:
- **AI Safe Acca** — target odds 1.8–2.3, max 2 legs, lowest risk.
- **AI Balanced Acca** — target odds 2.5–3.2, max 3 legs, moderate risk.

Acca generation must: avoid highly correlated legs · avoid duplicated match exposure · respect evidence thresholds · respect odds limits · respect operator availability.

## AI-5 — AI Explanation Engine
Every recommendation includes: Confidence · Evidence Strength · Supporting Factors · Risk Factors · Historical Similarity · Data Freshness · Odds Context. Natural-language explanations are generated by AI **only after** deterministic selection.

## AI-6 — AI Performance Dashboard
Publish measurable AI performance: Accuracy · ROI · Yield · Closing Line Value · Confidence Calibration · Model Drift · Prediction Distribution · League Performance · Market Performance. Transparency mandatory.

## AI-7 — "Why Not?"
For every rejected candidate, publish the rejection reason: Insufficient Evidence · Odds Outside Target Range · Conflicting Signals · Low Historical Reliability · Poor Data Quality · High Correlation · Insufficient Sample Size. Builds trust and creates unique SEO content.

## AI-8 — League Models (future)
Independent ranking models per league: Premier League · La Liga · Serie A · Bundesliga · Ligue 1 · MLS · etc.

## AI-9 — Market Models
Independent models per market: Over · BTTS · Corners · Cards · Home Win · Draw · Away Win.

## AI-10 — Operator Intelligence (future)
Use operator history to identify value opportunities, pricing differences, availability quality, and freshness quality — **without introducing bookmaker bias**.

# SEO Strategy

The AI layer generates high-quality, explainable football-intelligence pages. Primary targets: AI Bet of the Day · AI Match Prediction · AI Acca · Why This Pick · Why Not Selected · AI Confidence · Evidence Report · Historical Performance.

Every page must be indexable · contain structured evidence · support internal linking · support historical archives. Avoid thin AI-generated content · hallucinated analysis · duplicate explanations.

# Alignment Notes (non-binding; to revisit when the phase is authorized)

These observe how the vision maps onto the *already-built* evidence platform; they add no scope and authorize nothing.

- **Deterministic-selection / LLM-as-explainer** mirrors the platform's existing separation of concerns: frozen, deterministic capture (M6) and settlement (M8) logic vs. non-authoritative layers around them. AI-1's ranking engine should be a **pure, deterministic** scorer over already-qualified candidates — the LLM never enters the selection path.
- **Reproducibility** aligns with the immutable, content-hashed evidence snapshots and validation/settlement records: AI explanations should cite the specific snapshot / validation identities they derive from, so any recommendation is reproducible from historical evidence.
- **Fail-closed "No qualified Bet of the Day"** matches the platform's fail-closed discipline (e.g., strict readers surfacing absence honestly; settlement refusing bad input) — never fabricate a pick to fill a slot.
- **AI-7 "Why Not?"** can draw on the deterministic candidate-pipeline diagnostics (rejection reasons, deadline/ceiling deferrals, qualification outcomes) already produced upstream — rejection reasons should be sourced from that deterministic layer, not narrated by the LLM.
- **Data freshness** as an input connects to the still-deferred freshness-policy / partition-observability carry-forward in the M10 activation track — that groundwork would feed AI freshness signals rather than being re-invented here.
