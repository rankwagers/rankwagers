PART XX
HERO COMPONENT SPECIFICATION
20.0 Purpose

The Hero is the single most important component in RankWagers.

It is responsible for creating trust before users scroll.

The Hero does not advertise.

The Hero does not persuade.

The Hero demonstrates that meaningful football research has already been completed.

Within five seconds, users should understand:

Today's football has already been analysed.
Only the strongest research is visible.
Every recommendation is explainable.
This is not a betting homepage.
20.1 Success Criteria

A successful Hero should achieve the following:

Within 3 seconds:

Users recognise that research has already been completed.

Within 10 seconds:

Users understand why only selected fixtures are shown.

Within 15 seconds:

Users feel confident enough to begin exploring research.

The Hero succeeds when curiosity replaces scepticism.
